How is Colored Noise Used?

You can click on each of the audio files below to get a sample of each of the different colored noises. While you are listening, you can read about some of the common applications of different colored noise. Learn more about the science of colored noise applications from WebMD.




White noise has been associated with some benefits, including the following:

  • Masking unwanted sounds
  • Promoting relaxation
  • Improving sleep quality
  • Treating tinnitus
  • Managing ADHD symptoms

Pink noise has been associated with some benefits, including the following:

  • Promoting relaxation
  • Decreasing the time to fall asleep
  • Improving sleep depth and quality

Bornw noise has been associated with some benefits, including the following:

  • Treating tinnitus
  • Improving concentration
  • Relieving anxiety symptoms
