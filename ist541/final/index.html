Learning Outcomes

As a newly hired medical assistant, you are required to undergo basic health, safety, and privacy compliance training. This training is designed to guide you through the entire life cycle of a patient visit, beginning with patient check-in through preparing an exam room and disinfection and dispoal after the patient leaves. At the conclusion of this training, you will complete a certification quiz.


After viewing this website, you will be able to:

  • Recall which privacy practices are required under HIPPA

  • Explain common safety concerns when rooming a patient

  • Sequence the proper disposal and disinfection procedures for examination rooms.
